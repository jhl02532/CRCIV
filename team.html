Research Specialization

Insect Taste Receptors

Functional characterization of gustatory receptors in Apis mellifera, Apis cerana, and disease vectors.

Vector Control

Mosquito host-seeking and blood-feeding behavior studies for disease control.

Comparative Genomics

Genome sequencing and comparative analysis across insect species.

Olfaction

Peripheral and central olfactory systems in insects.

Biosensors & AI

Development of bioelectronic systems using insect receptors.
